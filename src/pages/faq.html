FAQ

Will the tooth powder stain my sink?

It can, because it uses natural earth-toned ingredients. Just rinse or wipe after brushing.

How often should I use it?

Twice daily or at least once at night after dinner. Avoid eating afterward.

Can kids use it?

Yes, with adult supervision. Do not dip the toothbrush directly into the jar — use a small spoon/dish.
